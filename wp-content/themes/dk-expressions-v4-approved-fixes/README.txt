DK EXPRESSIONS V4 — EDITORIAL & EDITABLE UPDATE
================================================

Version 1.11.1

This is the child theme for the installed DK Expressions V4 parent theme.
The parent dk-expressions-v4 theme must remain installed.

- Makes name, email, contact number and city mandatory on every native entry form.

- Makes the confirmation email subject and introductory message editable per competition.
- Adds a refined animated blue-neon treatment to bold website text.

- Adds Giveaways to the editable main navigation.
- Removes the obsolete DKX Experience menu entry while preserving DK Experience.
- Adds a responsive Clients & Brands logo wall to the Home page.
- Connects the logo wall to the existing DKX Clients & Brands post type.
- Uses each client record’s Featured Image as its displayed logo.
- Adds editable logo-wall headings, introduction and display quantity.
- A dedicated cinematic landing page at the root domain.

INSTALLATION
------------
1. Keep the parent dk-expressions-v4 theme installed.
2. Open WordPress > Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme.
3. Upload this ZIP and choose Replace current with uploaded.
4. Ensure DK Expressions V4 — Approved Fixes remains active.
5. Clear all WordPress, server/CDN and browser caches.
6. Open DK Experience and save the preferred content.

ARTICLE SETTINGS
----------------
Open DK Experience > Article archive and Related Stories to change:
- Articles displayed per archive page
- Number of Related Stories
- Related Stories heading
- Previous-story and next-story labels

WordPress marks an article as featured by making it a Sticky post. Featured
articles appear first and are not repeated among the newest articles.
